GameSearch Account Deletion

Request removal of your GameSearch account from the app or by email. Below is how to do it and what happens to your data.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open the app on your device and sign in if needed.
  2. Go to Settings from the main navigation or profile menu.
  3. Select Delete Account and follow the prompts to confirm.

Request by email

You can also ask us to delete your account by sending a message from the address associated with your account:

gamesearch16@gmail.com

When your account is deleted

  • Profile data is removed.
  • Comments and related content may be deleted or anonymized.
  • Some technical data may be retained temporarily for security and legal purposes.
